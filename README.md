# CTF Notes & Write-ups

A collection of notes from cybersecurity training rooms and Capture The Flag (CTF) exercises, focused on methodology, learning outcomes, and defensive takeaways.

> **Status:** Learning collection. Individual write-ups will be published after the notes have been checked and sanitised.

## Learning focus

- Linux fundamentals and command-line workflow
- Enumeration and information gathering
- Understanding common service and web-security weaknesses
- Basic privilege-escalation concepts
- Recording evidence and explaining the reasoning behind each step

## Practice milestones

The learning portfolio includes work on the TryHackMe rooms:

- Basic Pentesting
- Kenobi

A room being completed is a learning milestone; it should not be described as a professional penetration test or real-world engagement.

## Write-up format

Each write-up should contain:

1. **Challenge / room** — platform and challenge name.
2. **Scope** — the authorised lab environment.
3. **Objective** — what the challenge asks the learner to achieve.
4. **Methodology** — high-level sequence of investigation steps.
5. **Findings** — relevant evidence and explanations.
6. **Lessons learned** — concepts reinforced and mistakes corrected.
7. **Mitigations** — defensive controls relevant to the weakness.
8. **References** — official documentation and learning resources.

## Responsible disclosure and safety

All practical testing described here is limited to intentionally vulnerable labs, CTF platforms, or systems for which permission has been granted. Do not include real credentials, private keys, tokens, personal data, or flags where the platform rules prohibit publishing them.
